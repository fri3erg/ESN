🛂 Tandem Passport: pick a theme

Three looks for the same game. Everyone gets the same 6 missions, in a random order, one at a time. Each row shows the passport (your stamps), a new mission (with the conversation starter) and the screen you get after all 6 stamps (show a volunteer, enter the prize draw). Which one do you like: A, B or C?

A · Real passport

Navy and gold like a real passport, cream paper with a security pattern. Every quest you complete gets an ink stamp in a different colour, slightly crooked like at a border.

B · ESN bright

The ESN colours from the event guide: blue gradient header, clean white quest cards, magenta and green accents, a progress bar to the badge. Very readable, on-brand.

C · Sticker book

Sunny yellow and loud: every quest is a colourful sticker tile, the ones you finish get a "✓" sticker slapped on. Feels like a game, very party.
