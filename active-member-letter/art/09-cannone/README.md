# lettera-09-cannone

A circus human cannon, drawn with letters. A little performer in a cyan suit and a pink helmet waves,
runs up to the red and cream striped cannon, springs into a tucked somersault and lands wobbling on
the rim of the muzzle, then slides in feet first with arms up. A hand pops out to wave, ducks back in,
the fuse fizzles down, and BOOM: a burst of fire, smoke,
sparks and confetti, and they fly across the frame in a big arc, spinning and flailing, and land in the
safety net with their arms up and stars circling their head. Then they vanish in a sparkle and it all
starts again, about every 7 seconds.

It's one slide of my letter to ESN Bologna, the one where I put my head in the human cannon and aim at
tandem coordinator and webmaster. The cannon, the net and the flight path are worked out from the shape
of the screen, so the arc always fits, on a wide screen or a phone.

Everything is drawn from code, no images. Adding `?t=10` to the address jumps 10 seconds in.
