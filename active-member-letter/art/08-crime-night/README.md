# lettera-08-crime-night 🔍

One slide of my letter to ESN Bologna, the one about tandems and Crime Night, the noir detective game
I made for our tandem evenings. It's a big fingerprint lying on a dark desk, drawn with letters. The ridges
draw themselves from the middle outwards, then a magnifying glass slides in from the corner and sweeps over
the print in a slow figure of eight: under the lens the ridges are bigger and lit warm, everywhere else
they stay faint and grey. A lamp swings gently overhead, smoke drifts through its light, and two strips of
black and yellow police tape flutter across the corners.

No words anywhere, and everything is drawn from code (the fingerprint is a whorl made of noise and
spirals), so there are no image files. It restarts every time its slide opens: a couple of seconds of
intro, then it just keeps sweeping.

## putting it online

A normal Vercel import works: set **Root Directory** to `projects/lettera-08-crime-night`, framework
**Other**. Adding `?t=10` to the address jumps 10 seconds in.
