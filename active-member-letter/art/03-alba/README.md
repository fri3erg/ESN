# lettera-03-alba

The sun coming up over the sea, drawn with letters. It's one slide of my letter to ESN Bologna, the one
about learning to smile at people and be a kind face in a place where you don't know anyone yet. It comes
right after the cold rainy slide, so I wanted it to feel like light arriving.

It opens on a dark night sea with the last few stars. In the first couple of seconds the sun comes up out
of the water, only halfway, and the sky warms from blue to rose and gold. Then it just stays there, a big
half sun sitting on the sea: thin clouds drift by lit from below, soft rays turn very slowly, and the
sun's reflection shimmers on the waves as a broken column of gold.

No words and no image files, everything is drawn from code. Adding `?t=10` to the address jumps 10
seconds in.
