# lettera-06-limoncello

A short, round-shouldered bottle of limoncello, drawn with letters, turning slowly on the spot. It pops up from below, then the
music starts: on every beat (120 a minute) rings of sound pulse out to both sides in party colours and
the floor under it glows, while the bottle stays put, turning, with the limoncello sloshing gently inside. There's a lemon slice painted on the glass
that comes round as it turns, a light that glints off the glass, confetti drifting down, and every so
often a burst of confetti shoots out of the top.

It's one slide of my letter to ESN Bologna, the one about not expecting you: your personalities, the
limoncello, the party bags, the blessings. No words in the picture, the letter does the talking.

Everything is drawn from code, no images. Adding `?t=10` to the address jumps 10 seconds in.
