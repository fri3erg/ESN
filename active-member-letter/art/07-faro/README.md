# lettera-07-faro

A lighthouse on a rock at night, drawn with letters. It's one slide of my letter to ESN Bologna, the one
where I admit I can drown in trying to be the most useful, kind and perfect lighthouse for anyone who
talks to me.

The tower has red and white bands and a warm lamp that flickers on at the start. Then the beam turns
round and round: from the side it's a narrow cone sweeping across the night to the left or the right,
lighting up the mist, and as it swings towards you it opens into a wide warm flash. Where it falls on the
water a patch of glitter slides across the waves. A few stars, calm night blues, and the mist drifts
slowly.

No words and no image files, everything is drawn from code. Adding `?t=10` to the address jumps 10
seconds in.
