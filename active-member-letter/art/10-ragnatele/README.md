# lettera-10-ragnatele

An old dusty corner, drawn with letters. Cobwebs hang in the top corners (and a smaller one at the
bottom), swaying a little in a draught, with lumps of dust caught in them and specks floating through
the air. A little spider with big round eyes lets itself down on a thread, hangs there for a while, then
scrambles back up and does it again.

It's one slide of my letter to ESN Bologna: the bit where I suggest that maybe it's time to retire that
ancient Jupiter and the bank-transfer payments. Some things have been gathering cobwebs.

Everything is drawn from code, so there are no image files. Adding `?t=20` to the address jumps 20
seconds in.
