# lettera 11: un bacio 💋

The last slide of my letter to ESN Bologna. It's the kiss from `ricordi` on its own: me blowing a kiss,
spreading out from the hands, with hearts flying out and growing as they come towards you. Here it doesn't
dissolve at the end: it stays and the hearts keep coming.

## the photo

The page needs `5-bacio.jpg` in this folder (the same file as in `ricordi`). It's gitignored: deploy from
this folder with `npx vercel --prod`, or copy the folder with the photo wherever the letter lives.
