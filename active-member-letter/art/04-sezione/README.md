# lettera 04: la mia sezione 🫶

One slide of my letter to ESN Bologna, the one about my first section: small, a bit messy, very human.
It's me and my people on a metro, holding up the ESN flag, drawn as an impression like the photos in
`ricordi`: the five of us filled in with glowing outlines, everything else gone to black. It ripples out
from the star on the flag, then the flag keeps waving gently and sparks in the star's four colours drift
up from it.

The flag keeps its ESN lettering, the one exception to the letter's no-words rule. In this page's copy
of the photo my eyes are open (in the original I'm laughing them shut). It uses a finer grid than the other
pages, so the five faces keep their eyes and smiles.

## the photo

The page needs `sezione.jpg` in this folder. It's gitignored, so it never goes to GitHub: deploy from
this folder with `npx vercel --prod`, or copy the folder (photo included) wherever the letter lives.
