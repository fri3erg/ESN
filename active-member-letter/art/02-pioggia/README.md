# lettera-02-pioggia

Rain on a dark window, drawn with letters. Lots of small drops form on the glass and run down in
quick little hops, leaving thin wet trails that dry fast. Now and then a smaller drop catches up with
the one below and joins it. Soft, muted blues and a sprinkle of mist, nothing else.

It's one slide of my letter to ESN Bologna, the one where I admit I didn't join for a noble reason: I
wanted friends and an excuse to leave the house. It felt like a rainy-window kind of paragraph.

Everything is drawn from code, so there are no image files. Adding `?t=20` to the address jumps 20
seconds in.
