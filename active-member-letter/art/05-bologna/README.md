# lettera 05: bologna 🏰

One slide of my letter to ESN Bologna, the one where I admit Bologna scared me a bit. It's the first
scene of `ricordi` on its own: the march under the two towers, drawn from the top down, with the crowd
in colour, the flags waving, swifts circling the towers and the crowd jumping. Here it doesn't dissolve
at the end: it stays and keeps moving.

## the photo

The page needs `1-bologna.jpg` in this folder (the same file as in `ricordi`). It's gitignored: deploy
from this folder with `npx vercel --prod`, or copy the folder with the photo wherever the letter lives.
