🧶 Il filo invisibile: pick a theme

Three looks for the same game, built from your guide. Meet three different people: read the question together, both answer, and when you find something specific you have in common, both tap Thread found to tie a knot. Three knots = the ball of yarn is complete, show it at the ESN desk for a prize. Italian and English, switchable.

Each row shows the start screen, a question, and the completed ball of yarn. Which one do you like: A, B or C?

A · Embroidery

The colours of your guide (teal, cream, terracotta) on a dotted canvas. A red thread is stitched across the screen and gets a knot every time you find a connection. Calm and clean.

B · Neon thread

Dark like the bar, with a glowing pink-and-cyan thread that lights up a little more with every knot. The most "party" one.

C · Yarn pop

Bright and playful: sunny yellow, pink and aqua, thick outlines, colourful balls of yarn. The card tilts a bit like a sticker. The most cheerful one.

Mockup only: colours and texts can all change. The questions come from your 70-question deck.
