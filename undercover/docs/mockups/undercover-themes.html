🕶️ Undercover: pick a theme

Three looks for the same game. Everyone types the number on their post-it and gets a secret target (another number) with a secret mission from your list: a word to make them say, or something to make them do. You can't say the word yourself, and if they guess your mission it doesn't count. When the target falls for it, tap Eliminated and you get a new target.

Each row shows the start screen, your target, and the screen after an elimination. Every elimination is a ticket for the prize draw. Which one do you like: A, B or C?

A · Masquerade

A masked ball: deep purple and gold, elegant letters. Your target hides behind a golden mask with only their number on it; an elimination "unmasks" them.

B · Night vision

A secret agent's gadget: green night-vision screen with scan lines and a blinking REC. Your target is locked in the viewfinder.

C · Incognito

Silly disguise party: hot pink, yellow and aqua, the glasses-and-moustache 🥸. Your target wears a disguise with their number; eliminations say BUSTED. The most playful one.

Mockup only: colours and texts can all change.
