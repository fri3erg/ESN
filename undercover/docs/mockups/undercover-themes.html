🕵️ Undercover: pick a theme

Three looks for the same game. Everyone types the number on their post-it, gets a secret target (another number) and a secret mission: a word to make them say, or something to make them do (missions from your list). When the target falls for it, tap Eliminated and you get a new target. Each row shows the start screen, your target, and the screen after an elimination (sent to the prize draw).

Every elimination is a ticket for the prize draw. Which one do you like: A, B or C?

A · Secret dossier

Kraft folder, typewriter letters, red TOP SECRET stamps. Your target is a polaroid with only a number on it. Feels like a real spy file.

B · Neon night

Dark like the club, neon pink and green that glow. Your target sits in a sniper scope. The most "party" of the three.

C · Wanted poster

Bright comic book: yellow with halftone dots, red and blue, thick black outlines. Your target is a WANTED poster, eliminations go BOOM.

Mockup only: colours, texts and missions can all change.
